# Dishbee+ Pakistan · product brief

Purpose: Pakistan-first food ordering/delivery marketplace and restaurant vendor/rider operations SaaS.

Brand treatment: Use the Dishbee black-and-yellow brand direction for public and product UI, while preserving semantic status colours for preparation, delivery and payment states. Current source has an Inter-based UI; check existing components before changing theme tokens.

User experience commitments: Low-bandwidth mobile ordering, readable menus, meal and basket combinations, clear fees, live order status, vendor availability, rider pickup/dropoff and accurate address/payment retries. Build separate customer/vendor/rider/admin surfaces, never fake real-time order status or hide commissions and delivery costs.

Existing repository rules, user permissions, safety/compliance and business/legal policies override design examples. Do not claim untested capabilities or launched integrations.
